ATM Chat

Offline messenger for people nearby. Exchange text, voice messages, photos, videos, and files between nearby iPhones and iPads over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth — no servers, no accounts, no internet needed.

Features

Offline Messaging

No Wi-Fi network? No cellular? No problem. Messaging works entirely over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth directly between phones. Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth in Control Center; there's no network to join, so it works in Airplane Mode too.

Through Other Phones

A one-to-one message can reach someone out of your range through one ATM Chat phone in between, and Everyone Nearby posts are passed along up to five hops. Since 1.2, your phone sends your one-to-one messages around the phones of people you've blocked when another phone can carry them.

Rich Messages

Send text, photos, videos, voice messages, contacts, links, and files. A caption goes as its own message. Since 1.2: paste a copied photo, drag a photo, video or file onto a chat, and choose which details a shared contact includes. Select Text and Translate use iOS's own tools, which may send the text you choose to Apple, as the privacy policy explains; on iOS 18 and later, Settings → Download Languages for Translation asks iOS to download the languages ATM Chat suggests (normally your device's language and Arabic, or English if your device is set to Arabic), so that Translate can work offline between those two. Link previews are off until you turn on Settings → Privacy → Load Link Previews (in 1.1 and earlier they load automatically).

Delivery & Read Receipts

See when your messages are delivered and read in one-to-one chats. Since 1.2 you can turn off read receipts and the typing indicator; an attachment shows Delivered only once its file is on the other phone; with read receipts on, Read reaches the sender even if you read their message while they were out of range; and the chat list shows your last message's status, with a clock while it waits. Everyone Nearby posts don't have receipts, but Home shows a post that wasn't delivered.

Reactions & Replies

React to messages with emoji, swipe to reply with the message quoted, and tap a quote to jump to the original. Since 1.2, a reply to a photo or video shows a thumbnail, replies work with files, contacts and several photos, and you can forward a message to several people at once.

Offline Queue

Since 1.2, everything you send in a one-to-one chat waits when the other person is out of range — photos, videos, voice messages and files too — and goes when you're both nearby with ATM Chat open, for up to 3 days. Videos and anything over 700 KB wait for a Wi-Fi link. A chat says "Waiting for <name>" while something waits. (In 1.1, only texts and links wait.)

No Account Required

No sign-up, no email, no phone number. Your device is your identity. Just open the app, choose a name and start chatting.

Persistent History

Chat history (the latest 10,000 messages per chat) is saved on your device and included in iCloud or computer backups of your device if you make them. Pick up conversations even after people disconnect.

Delete What You Want

Delete for Everyone removes a message you sent in a one-to-one chat from the other phone too, after asking. Phones on 1.2 or later act on it only when it arrives over a direct connection, and since 1.2 it's sent again on your next direct connection, and on new ones for a week after that; if it never gets there, their copy stays. Since 1.2 you can also delete any message from your own phone, clear a chat, delete a whole chat, or use Settings → Erase All Chats & Media to remove every chat and its media from this phone at once (your name, settings and blocked list stay).

Auto-Delete

Set a one-to-one chat to delete new messages from your phone after 30 seconds, 5 minutes, 1 hour or 24 hours, or when you next open ATM Chat if it wasn't open then. It works on your phone only, and the other person isn't told.

Everyone Nearby

A public, text-only room anyone nearby with ATM Chat can read — no setup, no invites. Names aren't verified. Since 1.2: you agree to the room's rules before your first post, posts that may be offensive are hidden until you tap Show (checked on your phone), you can block a poster by long-pressing their post or message them privately from it, and your own posts show how many devices they went to. Don't want it? Turn off Settings → Privacy → Show Everyone Nearby: it asks first, then hides the room and deletes its posts from your phone; your phone still passes posts on for others.

Message Requests — new in 1.2

Messages from people you haven't written to or accepted are listed apart. Only the first alerts with sound, and they don't see your read receipts until you reply or accept. Accept, Block or Delete. Accept someone under New People Nearby before they write; opening a chat doesn't accept anyone.

Notification Controls

Choose alerts for Messages and for Photos, Videos & Files, a sound for Everyone Nearby, and whether alerts show message text (off by default). Someone You Know Is Nearby (off by default on a new install) tells you when someone you've chatted with comes into range while ATM Chat is in the background, at most once per person every 30 minutes. Reply from an alert, and mute any chat.

Save All Photos & Videos

Save any received photo or video to your library — or a whole chat's photos and videos in one tap from Chat Info; a second Save All skips what's already saved. Send up to 10 photos at once, auto-compressed for Bluetooth.

Chat Info — new in 1.2

Tap a chat's name for its settings, tips when someone's phone is found but won't link, and when they were last nearby (the chat's header shows it too). Edit Name gives someone a name only you see in ATM Chat; it's never sent to them, though a report you send names them by it. A contact who renames themselves shows their old name for up to a week.

See Who's Nearby — new in 1.2

Tap the connected count on Home for everyone connected and how (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or through another phone), with Keep Screen On and Invite People. Phones that are found but won't link say "Can't connect yet", with Connection Tips. In a crowd, New People Nearby folds into one row.

Ready for No Signal — new in 1.2

While your group still has signal, Home's "Ready for no signal?" card reminds you to keep ATM Chat open, offers Keep Screen On, and shows who you have a Bluetooth link with — the link that still works when a phone locks.

Search, Media & Drafts

Search a chat, or all chats from Home. Since 1.2: photos and videos open full screen and swipe through the chat's media, with Reply, Forward and Delete; voice messages can be played back before sending and at 1.5× or 2×; and what you were writing is kept as a draft when you leave a chat.

English & Arabic — new in 1.2

The whole app in English or Arabic, right to left. Settings → Language takes you to ATM Chat's page in the Settings app to switch.

Accessibility

VoiceOver reads each message as one item with an Actions rotor, and since 1.2 a new message that arrives while you're on the chat list shows as a banner, so VoiceOver can read it. Larger text sizes, Reduce Motion, filled Read ticks, and higher-contrast buttons and links in light and dark mode.

How to Use

Open the app — No sign-up needed. Choose a display name of up to 40 characters (nearby devices see it, even before you chat). First-run setup says up front that messages aren't end-to-end encrypted, and explains each permission before iOS asks.

Discover nearby devices — Keep Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on (no network needed). Other ATM Chat users nearby appear automatically, across a room or into nearby rooms.

Start chatting — Tap a person under New People Nearby. After you write to them, they're under Chats. Send text, photos, videos, voice messages, contacts or files.

Message requests — Someone you haven't written to or accepted who writes to you shows up under Message Requests.

Stay connected — Everything you send waits when the other person is away, and goes when you're both nearby with ATM Chat open, for up to 3 days.

Talk to everyone nearby — Tap "Everyone Nearby" at the top of the list to post in the public room that everyone nearby can read.

Customize — Set your display name and avatar in Settings. ATM Chat connects to nearby ATM Chat users automatically and sends your name and avatar to every device it connects to, not only the people you chat with, and nearby devices see your display name even before you chat. Your name is also part of how your phone appears to nearby devices, travels with the messages and Everyone Nearby posts you send, and is passed on by connected phones to their own neighbours — by phones on version 1.2 or later only while you're connected to them, while earlier versions also passed on the names of devices they had met before. Invite People (in Settings or on an empty Home) shares the App Store link.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need internet to use ATM Chat?

No. Messaging works entirely over local Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. The only thing the app itself can fetch from the internet is a link preview (automatic in version 1.1 and earlier; since 1.2, off unless you turn on Settings → Privacy → Load Link Previews). It's designed for situations where there's no internet — travel, events, remote areas, or anywhere you just don't want to depend on a cellular connection. Translate (in a message's long-press menu and Select Text's menu) and Look Up (in Select Text's menu) are iOS's own: Look Up sends what you look up to Apple, and Translate works offline once both languages are downloaded; before that, iOS may send the text you translate to Apple.

Does ATM Chat work on a flight or in Airplane Mode?

Yes. Turn Wi-Fi and Bluetooth back on in Control Center on both phones; you don't join any network. With Bluetooth alone, texts and small files go, but videos and anything over 700 KB wait for Wi-Fi.

How far away can another person be?

ATM Chat reaches across a room or into nearby rooms; walls, crowds and interference shorten its range. Beyond that, other phones help: a one-to-one chat can reach someone who is connected to a phone you're connected to, which passes your messages along, and Everyone Nearby posts are passed along up to five hops.

Do messages arrive when my phone is locked?

Only over a Bluetooth link that's already connected, because iOS pauses ATM Chat's Wi-Fi in the background. Keep ATM Chat open, or turn on Settings → Keep Screen On. A reply you type into an alert goes the same way; without a Bluetooth link it waits, marked "Waiting for <name>", until you open ATM Chat.

How do I get ready before we lose signal?

While you're together and still have signal, open ATM Chat on everyone's phone and check Home's "Ready for no signal?" card: keep ATM Chat open, turn on Keep Screen On, and check who you have a Bluetooth link with (it's the link that still works when a phone locks). Invite anyone who doesn't have ATM Chat yet: nobody can download it once you're offline. On iOS 18 and later, also download translation languages in Settings (Download Languages for Translation).

Can I send photos and videos offline?

Yes. Photos, videos, voice messages, contacts, and files travel between devices over the local connection, never through a server, and since 1.2 they wait on your phone when the person is away. Videos need a direct Wi-Fi connection; over Bluetooth alone or through another phone, each file can be up to 700 KB (photos from your library are compressed to help them fit). You can untick any detail of a shared contact, or its photo, before you send it; the name always goes.

Why is a chat under Message Requests, and will it make a sound?

Someone you haven't written to or accepted sent you a message. Only their first message alerts with sound; later ones are silent, and they can't see you've read them until you reply or accept. You can accept, block or delete it. To accept a friend before they write, choose Accept on their row under New People Nearby, or in their empty chat; just opening their chat doesn't accept them. Deleting a chat with no message of yours left in it makes them a request again; deleting one that still has one keeps them accepted.

Someone's nearby but we won't connect. What can I do?

Their row says "Can't connect yet", and Chat Info and the connected count's sheet show tips under "Can't Connect". Ask them to keep ATM Chat open on screen; if you still don't connect, turn on Local Network for ATM Chat in Settings on both devices. Keep Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on in Control Center.

Can I erase all my chats at once?

Yes: Settings → Erase All Chats & Media deletes every chat, every Everyone Nearby post and their photos, videos and files from this phone, after asking. Your name, photo, settings and blocked list stay. It can't delete copies on other people's phones, and photos you saved to Photos stay there.

Can I hide Everyone Nearby?

Yes: turn off Settings → Privacy → Show Everyone Nearby (on by default). It asks before deleting the room's posts from your phone. Your phone still passes posts on for people nearby.

Is ATM Chat in Arabic?

Yes, when your iPhone, or ATM Chat in iOS Settings, is set to Arabic. ATM Chat's Settings → Language takes you there; if Language isn't listed, add a second language in Settings → General → Language & Region. This website and the privacy policy are in English.

Is the communication secure?

Messages travel between nearby devices over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth and never go through the internet or a server. They are not encrypted, though: transport-layer encryption is not enabled on the local link (a deliberate trade-off for reliable delivery) and there is no end-to-end encryption. Within radio range what you send isn't protected, and a phone that passes your message along receives it in readable form — the ATM Chat app doesn't show one-to-one messages it relays for others, but a modified app could. Treat chats like a conversation in a shared space, avoid sending secrets such as passwords, and keep the app updated for the latest security fixes. (iOS's Translate and Look Up: see Privacy below.) Blocking someone, from their chat, a message request or their Everyone Nearby post, drops their messages on your device and hides them from your lists, and what reached you during a block stays discarded after you unblock them. It doesn't disconnect them: they can still receive your name, avatar and Everyone Nearby posts. Your phone sends your one-to-one messages around theirs when another phone can carry them, but it can't control where another phone passes them next. You can also report a message or a person to us by email.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-up, email, or phone number required. Your device is your identity.

Privacy

ATM Chat has no servers. Messages travel between nearby devices — sometimes passed along by another ATM Chat user's phone — and never through a server. Chat history and media are stored on your device and, like other app data, are included in iCloud or computer backups of your device if you make them; the app never sends any of it to us. Translate (in a message's long-press menu and Select Text's menu) and Look Up (in Select Text's menu) are iOS's own: Look Up sends what you look up to Apple, and Translate works offline once both languages are downloaded; before that, iOS may send the text you translate to Apple. Nothing is collected: the only things we receive are emails people choose to send us, such as a report. A report includes the reported person's name and device ID and, for a reported message, its sender's name and device ID and its text; each name is the one you see in ATM Chat, which can be a name you gave them. The app itself goes online only to load link previews. In version 1.1 and earlier a preview loads automatically when a link appears in a chat, which tells that website your IP address; since version 1.2 previews are off unless you turn on Settings → Privacy → Load Link Previews.
